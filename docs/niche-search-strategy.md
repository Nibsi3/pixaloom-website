# Pixaloom niche search strategy — 7 October 2026

## Decision and evidence limits

Lead with **technical product catalogues for South African suppliers**, supported by the existing NORDflam and BuildVolume interface studies. Develop **Supabase login/data diagnosis** and **preview-to-production repair** as two focused parts of the existing AI rescue service. These are testable positioning choices, not established search-volume or ranking conclusions.

The public-web review found competitors in all three areas. It did not access Pixaloom's current Search Console performance, enquiries, field Core Web Vitals or a Google search-position tracker. Search results from the research tool are discovery evidence, not measurements of Google's current South African positions. Exact search volume, difficulty, conversion rate and commercial return remain unknown. The Tavily Pro research request exceeded the connected plan's usage limit; manual web searches and primary-page checks continued.

Pixaloom cannot make a defensible promise to be first on Google or prevent others from competing. Its opportunity is to make the offer more useful and provable for a specific buyer, then measure which pages attract suitable work. Keep the broad existing services; these pages explain distinct workflows within them.

Each new page has substantive task-specific content, a decision/checkpoint table, real relevant project links, explicit scope boundaries and an ungated project-brief builder. The brief is self-reported and remains in browser component state. It does not scan a site, certify readiness, collect data in the background or send an enquiry. Ordinary accessible HTML includes all check labels, explanations and contact links before JavaScript executes.

The services index, existing ecommerce evidence and AI rescue pages provide contextual discovery links. New URLs appear in the sitemap with individually maintained modification dates, self-canonical metadata, a visible/structured breadcrumb and Service/WebPage markup. No FAQ rich-result, keyword-density or schema-ranking promise is made. No new towns, invented offices, platform partnerships, client results or paid links are introduced.

## Build a defensible advantage

1. **Catalogue:** with client permission, publish one detailed model/category decision record using authentic screenshots, a redacted sample-data mapping and the actual enquiry handoff. State what Pixaloom delivered and what it did not. A price-feed/stock claim needs operational evidence before publication.
2. **Rescue:** after a real, permissioned engagement, record the original symptom, the scope, redacted change evidence and repeatable acceptance tests. Separate targeted repair from a comprehensive security assessment. Do not re-label Illumi as a rescued Lovable app.
3. **Reference assets:** maintain the ungated planning checklists as the platforms change. Add a downloadable supplier-data template or redacted diagnostic example only when it reflects a real delivery process. More pages are not automatically more authority.
4. **Trust:** obtain genuine references and accurate relevant business listings. Link requests need a natural editorial reason and authorisation. This change sends no outreach, buys no links and manufactures no reviews.

Original delivery evidence, accurate explanations and repeatable acceptance criteria can take time to reproduce. The phrasing of a niche offer alone is easy for a competitor to copy.

## Publication and measurement

Local validation on Node.js 22 passed the production build, TypeScript, ESLint and all 52 regression tests. The production-build crawler checked 61 sitemap pages, 61 unique internal links and 79 images with zero failures or warnings. Chromium checks exercised all three brief tools at 1440px and 390px: selection updates, successful clipboard copy, denied-copy fallback and reset on reload passed, with no viewport overflow or JavaScript console errors. These are local release checks, not live deployment or real-user performance measurements.

First deploy the reviewed commit to the existing Cloudflare Worker, then run the live SEO crawl and verify receiving-inbox delivery separately. Existing shell restrictions currently block the deployment API and production hosts, so local validation does not establish publication. Preserve the current mail routing and dashboard-managed secrets.

After publication, inspect the three URLs in Search Console, confirm the canonical and index status, and keep only the intended sitemap submission. Record observed status separately from any indexing request. Neither a submission nor a successful live test establishes ranking.

Use the first comparable 28-day period after indexing as an initial observation window, then compare successive 28-day and three-month windows. Export privately: query, page, date, country, device, impressions, clicks, CTR and average position. Separate branded and client-brand queries from non-brand buyer searches. Review whether multiple Pixaloom URLs receive the same intent before creating another page. Search Console average position is not a universal rank for every searcher.

Track qualified catalogue enquiries and suitable rescue diagnoses against completed conversations. A brief-copy click, a contact-link click or a provider-accepted email is not a qualified lead. Preserve the site's existing privacy constraints and choose any analytics destination and retention policy before adding tracking. No new tracking destination is added in this release.

Keep pages when their content helps buyers even during a low-impression period. Revise positioning based on actual mismatches or enquiry quality; do not manufacture low-competition claims from missing data. Prioritise proven demand if Search Console and enquiry records show another niche performs better.
