Published route: `/custom-website-vs-website-builder`.

configuration and ongoing work. A second WooCommerce comparison was deliberately
omitted because the existing Next.js/WordPress and ecommerce planning guides
already cover the technical and operational decisions. The new page links to both.

  storefront approaches. No platform prices, performance or sales claims are reused.

  backups, updates and plugin/theme troubleshooting responsibilities.
