# SEO maintenance and owner checklist

## September 2026 remediation

This release improves discoverability, honest content, internal linking and enquiry reliability without replacing the three featured homepage projects or inventing testimonials and commercial results.

Implemented: safe article formatting and contextual links; revised copy for all 16 guides; service proof and budget boundaries across five services; distinct planning examples across nine province pages; clearer George/Garden Route content; named editorial contact; privacy notice; per-page modification dates; preview noindex; robots/noindex alignment; removal of an unrelated redirect and a broken paused-project link; estimator context and included-payment handling; configuration-aware contact fallback and validated API; identifier-free operational events; persistent hero offer and motion controls; verified gallery descriptions; removal of 83 unreferenced CSS rules (10,146 source bytes, not a network-transfer claim).

All 66 previously rendered gallery captures were visually reviewed. Three were excluded from public galleries: Paws' empty about-image area, its placeholder contact page, and Covercrete's empty shop. Source files remain in the repository. The other 63 have descriptions based on visible content, including files whose names do not match their pictured page. Original captures are reused in relevant articles; no synthetic client imagery was created.

## Owner-dependent completion gates

1. **Delivered enquiries:** configure the real email credential and verified sender, then authorise one test to the business inbox. Check acceptance and actual delivery separately. Configure an alerting destination if automated failure alerts are required.
2. **Qualified leads:** select an analytics destination, retention policy and access owner. Define a qualified lead and reconcile it with actual enquiries; a beacon click alone is not evidence. Keep names, emails, messages, query strings and visitor identifiers out of action-event payloads.
3. **Business Profile:** check account status and eligibility. Confirm whether customers visit a staffed location, whether the business visits customers, or whether it is online-only. Use accurate real-world details. Resolve any suspension through Google's official process with genuine evidence; do not create a replacement to bypass enforcement or invent offices, hours or documents.
4. **Portfolio authority:** confirm each project role, version, client permission and reference before publishing an endorsement. Obtain permissioned before/after evidence with dates and measurement conditions. Screenshots and demo counters are not validated outcomes.
5. **Privacy:** the notice describes implemented processing, not a legal certification. The owner should verify mailbox access, retention, cross-border arrangements and applicable obligations with an appropriate adviser.
6. **Editorial accountability:** Cameron Falck is the studio contact, not an assertion that he personally reviewed every revision. Arrange subject-matter review and add a reviewer credit only after that review occurs.

## Earned authority plan

- Identify completed, permissioned projects whose owners can discuss the work. Record actual contributions, dates and reference URLs where appropriate.
- Ask for honest references or case-study permission; do not demand keyword-rich links, buy ranking links or invent reviews. No outreach is sent automatically by this release.
- Publish a useful implementation write-up with authentic screenshots, scope boundaries and reproducible measurements before increasing frequency.
- Correct existing legitimate listings after the owner confirms the operating model and contact details. Keep old and corrected values privately.
- Assess relevant mentions and qualified enquiries over time, not raw link counts alone.

## Release and recrawl acceptance

- Tests, lint, typecheck and production build pass. Crawl the canonical site after hosting completes.
- Sitemap URLs return 200 with unique titles/descriptions, self-canonicals and one H1. Intended 404s stay excluded.
- Robots permits discovery of noindex pages. Preview `X-Robots-Tag` must not appear on production.
- Fingerprinted JS/CSS uses immutable caching; API responses use no-store.
- Test the privacy link on narrow screens, estimator handoff, first-frame offer and pause/play. Check reduced-motion preferences.
- Retest representative pages with PageSpeed. Lab results do not substitute for field data or INP.
- Keep only intentional Search Console sitemap submissions. Inspect priority URLs after deployment; record stored status separately from live-test and request status. A request is not confirmation of indexing. Historical reports require recrawl time; intentional noindex/404s are not errors to conceal.

## Primary guidance

- [Google Search documentation updates](https://developers.google.com/search/updates): current supported search features and retired FAQ rich results.
- [Business Profile representation guidelines](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177): eligibility, names, addresses and service areas.
- [Core Web Vitals](https://web.dev/articles/vitals): field metrics, distinct from a single Lighthouse score.
- [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/): accessibility, motion and keyboard interaction.
- [Information Regulator](https://inforegulator.org.za/): South African privacy information.

Keep account screenshots, search-performance exports, business documents and correspondence out of public version control.
